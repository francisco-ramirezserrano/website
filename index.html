About me

I've lived in Costa Rica, Senegal, Benin, Italy, Germany, Switzerland, the UK and the US, and I'm Spanish and Swiss. Moving that much made me an empiricist: I don't really believe something until I've tried it myself.

I studied microengineering at EPFL in Lausanne, with an exchange year at TUM in Munich, and wrote my master's thesis in robotics at Harvard. I stayed on as a research fellow, worked at Nothing in London, and started my PhD at MIT in 2024.

At MIT I work with humanoid robots and think a lot about how AI should be used around people; I was an MIT SERC Scholar on exactly that. I also teach, guiding students through their first research projects.

Outside work I'm usually on the water wingfoiling or surfing, playing ping-pong, deep in a game of Dune: Imperium with friends, or reading philosophy and psychology.

I speak Spanish, English, French, German and Italian.
